02 / SOUND
SOUND

Sound is the part of Aku no Hana that gives sound to feeling and feeling to sound, tracing what happens when rhythm, machinery and the body begin to occupy the same space.

PHILOSOPHY

Repetition is everywhere. In breath, machinery, memory, ritual, anxiety, the pulse beneath the skin, the electrical hum of a room we have stopped hearing. Repeat something long enough, and it begins to alter the body receiving it, settling gradually into muscle, memory and expectation until what began outside of us becomes difficult to separate from what is happening within us, and the rhythm produced by the machine is no longer merely something we hear but something the body anticipates, carries and eventually reproduces.

Aku no Hana captures the sound that lives between analogue and digital, in the electrical hum of machines, the pulse beneath the skin and the repetitions that pass through the body until they begin to settle there. The body repeats what the machine remembers, slowly becoming an archive of everything that has passed through it, until it begins to reproduce what it has heard. We create machines in our image, teach them our language, our repetitions and our desires, and then live long enough beside them for the exchange to reverse, until the human begins to change alongside what it has created and language itself becomes contaminated with digital hums. The human creates the machine that, in turn, redefines the human, while the distinction between what originates in the body and what has been learned from the machine becomes increasingly difficult to locate.

A groove can hold contradiction for longer than a sentence, allowing the mechanical and the intimate, pleasure and tension, repetition and mutation to coexist without demanding resolution. Aku no Hana carries the sound of the soul of the machine and the hum of the body echoing in a permanent feedback loop, a symbiotic conversation, like bacteria in the gut, living together, altering one another, neither remaining entirely what it was before.
