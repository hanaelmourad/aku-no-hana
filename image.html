03 / IMAGE
IMAGE

Image is where the human and the machine begin to lose their clean edges. Moving image, found footage, generated material and digital manipulation become ways of testing what changes when an image passes through a machine.

HUMAN / MACHINE

I am interested in authorship as something unstable. An image can begin in a human imagination, pass through a generative system and return looking both familiar and foreign.

The work follows that uncertainty: who made the image, where intention sits, and whether knowing that a machine participated changes what we feel when we encounter it.
